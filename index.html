A BETTER STARTING POINT

Launch less. Reach more.

01

Keep essentials within one click.

Open your most-used apps, folders, documents, URLs, and commands from a menu that lives exactly where you expect it: the taskbar.

02

Make the menu yours.

Arrange launch items into groups, choose their order, and tune the menu to the way you work—not the other way around.

03

Bring desktop and web together.

Discover supported Windows apps and browser PWAs, then add the ones you choose to the same focused launch menu.

04 A TOOL THAT STAYS OUT OF THE WAY

Useful without becoming another service.

PinLaunch is a local Windows application. Its configuration belongs on your device, and its purpose is to get you to your tools—not to get between you and them.

PRIVACY

Nothing leaves your device.

没有任何信息离开你的设备。

PinLaunch does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. Menu configuration and shortcut or app discovery are processed locally on your device only.

PinLaunch does not include telemetry, analytics, advertising, account sign-in, cloud sync, or automatic update checking.

If a future version adds a network or data-collection feature, this statement will be updated before that version is released.
